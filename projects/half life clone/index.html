Franchise History & Lore

The Half-Life Saga: From Black Mesa to the Resistance

In 1998, Half-Life revolutionized interactive storytelling and the first-person shooter genre at the subterranean Black Mesa Research Facility in New Mexico. When theoretical physicist Gordon Freeman pushed an anomalous crystal into the anti-mass spectrometer, the catastrophic Resonance Cascade tore a rift between dimensions, unleashing hostile alien species from Xen.

Following Earth's swift defeat in the devastating Seven-Hour War, the multidimensional Combine empire occupied the planet. Earth became a dystopian police state centered around City 17, dominated by the colossal Citadel tower and the oppressive Combine Overwatch surveillance network under Administrator Dr. Wallace Breen.

Emerging from stasis twenty years later, Gordon Freeman joins forces with resistance fighter Alyx Vance and her loyal robotic protector Dog. Together, armed with the iconic HEV Suit, the legendary crowbar, and the Zero Point Energy Field Manipulator (Gravity Gun), they spark the human resistance rebellion to reclaim humanity's future from alien subjugation.
