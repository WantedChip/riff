Dystopian Setting

City 17 & The Citadel Occupation

Awakened from stasis by the enigmatic G-Man, Gordon Freeman arrives by train in City 17, an Eastern European metropolis transformed into the totalitarian hub of the Combine's planetary occupation.

Looming over the cityscape is the monolithic Combine Citadel, where Administrator Dr. Wallace Breen negotiates humanity's compliance. Freeman links up with the underground Resistance, including Barney Calhoun, Dr. Eli Vance, Dr. Isaac Kleiner, and Eli's daughter Alyx Vance.

From the zombie-infested alleys of Ravenholm to the fortified coastal highway and the perilous assault on Nova Prospekt, Gordon leads an all-out uprising to bring down the Citadel from the inside.

The Gravity Gun Innovation

The Zero Point Energy Field Manipulator (Gravity Gun) allows players to grab, hold, manipulate, and launch heavy physical objects as deadly projectiles. From hurling circular saw blades in Ravenholm to constructing barricades against Combine assault gunships, physics became an organic weapon and puzzle tool.

Technological Mastery

Source Engine Physics, AI & Facial Animation

Havok Physics Simulation

Real-time rigid-body dynamics, buoyant fluid displacement, structural balance, and kinematic ragdoll reactions bring every crate, barrel, and vehicle to life.

Facial Expression System

40 independent muscle facial flex controllers synthesize nuanced emotional expressions, lip sync, and eye gaze tracking across all story characters.

Advanced Shaders & Lighting

Radiosity normal mapping, dynamic high dynamic range (HDR) exposure simulation, and anisotropic water surface reflections set new visual standards in 2004.
