Half-Life: Alyx

VR EXCLUSIVE STEAMVR VALVE INDEX

Playing as Alyx Vance, you are humanity’s only chance for survival. The Combine’s control of the planet since the Black Mesa incident has only strengthened as they corral the remaining population in cities. Among them are some of Earth’s greatest scientists: you and your father, Dr. Eli Vance.

Virtual Reality Innovation

Built Exclusively for Virtual Reality Mechanics & Physics

Room-Scale Interaction

Lean around a broken wall and under a Barnacle to make an impossible shot. Scour shelves to find a syringe and some shotgun shells. Manipulate tools to hack alien interfaces.

Tactile Combat & Reloading

Tear a Headcrab off your face and throw it out the window. Manually eject spent magazines, load fresh cartridges, and rack your slide in intense fire fights against Combine soldiers.

Full Physics Simulation

Powered by Valve's next-generation Source 2 engine, every object in the world has physical mass, momentum, collision friction, and tactile responsiveness.

Signature Weaponry

The Gravity Gloves (The Russells)

Invented by eccentric resistance scientist Russell, the Gravity Gloves allow Alyx to magnetically target, lock onto, and flick distant objects and ammo directly into her hands with precision.

They serve as Alyx's essential survival tool, health status monitor, ammo counter, and resin accumulator throughout the Quarantine Zone.
