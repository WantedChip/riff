The Cataclysmic Meltdown

Citadel Core Containment & City 17 Evacuation

Following the destruction of the dark energy teleport at the apex of the Citadel, the structural integrity of the entire monolith begins to fail. The reactor core is going critical, threatening to vaporize City 17 in a catastrophic nuclear-scale explosion.

Gordon and Alyx must brave the radioactive lower depths of the Citadel to temporarily stabilize the containment core, buying vital hours for the citizens and Resistance members to board evacuation trains before total annihilation.

Along the way, they discover Combine Advisor pods and intercept an encrypted transmission containing planetary Combine portal coordinates.

Alyx Vance: Cooperative Companion AI

Episode One introduced a groundbreaking partner AI in Alyx Vance. She provides covering fire with her signature automatic pistol, navigates pitch-black underground parking garages using Freeman's flashlight beam, hacks Combine security rollout balls with her EMP multi-tool, and reacts dynamically to player tactical decisions.

New Threat Spotlight

The Zombine (Combine Zombie Encounter)

Parasitic Infection

Combine Overwatch infantry soldiers infected by mutated necrotic Headcrabs inside the crumbling Citadel ruins.

Grenade Kamikaze Tactics

Unlike sluggish standard zombies, the Zombine can sprint forward and prime armed live grenades in close-quarters kamikaze charges.

Gravity Gun Counterplay

Players can use the Gravity Gun to snatch the live grenade directly from the Zombine's decaying hand and fling it back into enemy ranks.
